EXPLORE 716

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Explore 716 is an Android app for recording outings and exploring Buffalo and its surrounding communities. This policy describes how the app uses location, what it saves, and the outside services involved. Questions can be sent to savicreationsny@gmail.com.

In brief: Explore 716 works without an account or internet connection. Routes and progress remain in the app's local database. If cloud backup is available in your build and you choose to enable it, selected progress records are also stored in Firebase so you can restore them on another phone. Detailed raw GPS routes are not included in Explore 716 cloud backup.

Location and Explore sessions

With your permission, the app uses precise or approximate device location to show your position, offer nearby destinations, find a starting point for an Area Drive, and record an Explore session. An Explore session begins only when you select Start Explore and ends when you select Stop. During an active session, location can continue to be processed while the screen is off or the app is in the background; Android displays a persistent tracking notification.

Accepted location points form your saved route. The app uses those points on the device to update exploration-area progress and validate nearby Discoveries. It also keeps session times, route segments, completion records, and drive history. Location accuracy and system behavior can affect what is recorded.

Local storage and optional cloud backup

Explore 716 stores routes, sessions, exploration-area progress, Discovery status, and related processing state in SQLite on your device. Maps and routing reference data are packaged with the app. You can use Explore, Destinations, Area Drives, and all progress views without signing in.

If you choose cloud backup, Firebase Authentication processes your account email address or Google sign-in identity and an account identifier. Cloud Firestore stores completed exploration zones, Discovery completion and removal states, and stopped-drive summaries such as dates, duration, distance, and discoveries found. These records can reveal places you visited. The app retains a local queue of changes while offline and sends them after connectivity returns. Detailed raw GPS points, route geometry, and field diagnostics are not sent to Explore 716 cloud backup.

Field-diagnostics builds may keep a small rolling history of tracking and recovery events on the device. The app does not automatically upload those events to us. The normal app does not include an advertising or app-analytics integration.

Google Maps and other services

The in-app map uses the Google Maps SDK. Google says this SDK automatically receives request and device metadata, an IP address, a Maps SDK-specific pseudonymous identifier, and SDK crash metrics or stack traces. Depending on use of the map camera, it may also receive map interactions such as pans and zooms. Google uses this information to operate and improve its services. The app does not deliberately upload its saved route database or Discovery records to Google Maps.

If you choose Open in Google Maps for an Area Drive, the app passes the route's destination and optional waypoints, and when appropriate your current starting coordinate, to the Google Maps app or website. Google Maps then handles navigation under Google's Privacy Policy. See Google's Maps SDK data disclosure for its current SDK practices.

Android's optional device backup can include app data, including a local database, in a backup associated with your Google account. Backup and restore are controlled by Android and your device settings, not by an Explore 716 account. See Google's Android backup information.

Collection and sharing

We do not sell your data or run ads. Google receives the Maps SDK information described above when the in-app map is used. If you enable cloud backup, Google Firebase processes your account and the selected progress records above on our behalf. We use those records only to back up and restore your Explore 716 progress. See the Firebase privacy information and Google Privacy Policy. Opening Google Maps is a separate user-initiated handoff. If you email us, your email provider and ours process that message; we use it to respond.

This website is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no analytics scripts or site tracking cookies. GitHub may process requests to serve it under its privacy statement.

Security and encryption

SQLite data is kept in Android app storage and is not separately encrypted by Explore 716. Device protections and Android backup encryption depend on your settings. Firebase transfers backup data over encrypted connections and controls access with account-specific Firestore rules. We do not claim end-to-end encryption of the backup. This website uses HTTPS. Google states that Google Maps Platform supports encryption in transit.

Retention, deletion, and your controls

Stopping Explore ends that session's location tracking; it does not erase its saved route or earned progress. You can revoke location permission in Android Settings. To remove local records, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android device backup may separately retain eligible local data according to your Google account settings.

Signing out stops new Explore 716 cloud backup activity and keeps local progress on the phone. In Account & Backup, you can delete your Firebase account and its cloud progress. The app requires a recent sign-in, an internet connection, and time to remove and verify cloud backup records before it deletes the account. If the process is interrupted, sign in and resume it. You can request manual help in the app or on our account-deletion request page if you cannot finish. We do not promise unattended deletion if the app is abandoned. Local progress remains on the phone unless you clear app data; a minimal deletion marker remains in Firestore until we remove it manually, to prevent an old device from recreating the backup.

Children's privacy

Explore 716 is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us to request its deletion.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the app, its services, or its data practices change. The date at the top shows the latest update. For privacy questions, email savicreationsny@gmail.com.
