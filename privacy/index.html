EXPLORE 716

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Explore 716 is an Android app for recording outings and exploring Buffalo and its surrounding communities. This policy describes how the app uses location, what it saves, and the outside services involved. Questions can be sent to savicreationsny@gmail.com.

In brief: Explore routes, progress, and Discoveries are saved in the app's local database. We do not operate an account or a server that receives those saved records. Google Maps and Android device backup are separate services described below.

Location and Explore sessions

With your permission, the app uses precise or approximate device location to show your position, offer nearby destinations, find a starting point for an Area Drive, and record an Explore session. An Explore session begins only when you select Start Explore and ends when you select Stop. During an active session, location can continue to be processed while the screen is off or the app is in the background; Android displays a persistent tracking notification.

Accepted location points form your saved route. The app uses those points on the device to update exploration-area progress and validate nearby Discoveries. It also keeps session times, route segments, completion records, and drive history. Location accuracy and system behavior can affect what is recorded.

Local storage and diagnostics

Explore 716 stores routes, sessions, area progress, Discovery status, and related processing state in SQLite on your device. Maps and routing reference data are packaged with the app. The app does not require an account or send saved Explore history to a developer-operated backend.

Field-diagnostics builds may also keep a small rolling history of tracking and recovery events on the device. The app does not automatically upload those events to us. The normal app does not include an advertising or app-analytics integration.

Google Maps and other services

The in-app map uses the Google Maps SDK. Google says this SDK automatically receives request and device metadata, an IP address, a Maps SDK-specific pseudonymous identifier, and SDK crash metrics or stack traces. Depending on use of the map camera, it may also receive map interactions such as pans and zooms. Google uses this information to operate and improve its services. The app does not deliberately upload its saved route database or Discovery records to Google Maps.

If you choose Open in Google Maps for an Area Drive, the app passes the route's destination and optional waypoints, and when appropriate your current starting coordinate, to the Google Maps app or website. Google Maps then handles navigation under Google's Privacy Policy. See Google's Maps SDK data disclosure for its current SDK practices.

Android's optional device backup can include app data, including a local database, in a backup associated with your Google account. Backup and restore are controlled by Android and your device settings, not by an Explore 716 account. See Google's Android backup information.

Collection and sharing

We do not sell your data, run ads, or maintain a server-side copy of your routes or progress. Google receives the SDK information described above when the in-app map is used. Opening Google Maps is a user-initiated handoff. If you email us, your email provider and ours process that message; we use it to respond to you.

This website is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not include analytics scripts or set its own tracking cookies. GitHub may process requests to serve the site under its privacy statement.

Security and encryption

The app's SQLite data is kept in Android app storage and is not separately encrypted by Explore 716. Device protections and any Android backup encryption depend on your device and account settings. This website uses HTTPS. Google states that Google Maps Platform supports encryption in transit for its services. We do not claim that the app's local records are end-to-end encrypted.

Retention, deletion, and your controls

Stopping Explore ends that session's location tracking; it does not erase its saved route or earned progress. You can revoke location permission in Android Settings, but that does not delete records already saved. To remove local Explore 716 data, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. An Android backup may persist or restore eligible data according to your backup settings; manage or delete that backup through your Google account and device settings.

Because we do not maintain an Explore 716 account or a route-history server, we cannot delete a local database remotely. To ask about information you have sent us by email, contact savicreationsny@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Explore 716 is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly operate an account or collect children's Explore history on a developer server. If you have a concern, contact us at the address below.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the app, its services, or its data practices change. The date at the top shows the latest update. For privacy questions, email savicreationsny@gmail.com.
