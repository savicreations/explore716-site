EXPLORE 716

Delete your account and cloud backup

You can delete your Explore 716 account and its cloud progress in the app: open Account & Backup, choose Delete cloud backup / account, and follow the sign-in confirmation. Keep the app open and online until deletion is confirmed. If interrupted, sign in and choose Resume account deletion. If you cannot sign in or finish in the app, use its Request account deletion help option or email savicreationsny@gmail.com from the address used for your account. Include only that account email and your request. Do not send a password, location history, or identity document.

What is removed

The request covers your Firebase Authentication account and Explore 716 cloud backup records: completed zones, Discovery state, and stopped-drive summaries. We may need to verify account ownership before processing an email request. Our support team handles requests that cannot finish in the app; no unattended server deletion is promised. A minimal deletion marker remains until manual cleanup to prevent another signed-in device from recreating the backup.

What stays on your phone

Deleting the cloud account does not automatically erase SQLite data on the phone. To remove local routes and progress, clear Explore 716 app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Android device backup is separate and controlled through your Google account settings.

Questions

Contact savicreationsny@gmail.com. See the Privacy Policy for more detail.
